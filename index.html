A little about me

Learn with a teacher who makes it practical.

你好

Hi, I'm Chun, a Chinese language teacher. I help learners make sense of Chinese and use it with greater confidence in real-life situations.

My lessons focus on clear explanations, useful vocabulary and natural expressions. Whether you're starting from zero or looking to improve your fluency, I want to make your learning journey structured, engaging and achievable.

My teaching approach: learn useful language, understand how it works, and put it into practice.

Curious about Chinese culture? I also offer intro session on Bazi(Chinese astrology) and traditional philosopy-a fun add-on to your language journey.
