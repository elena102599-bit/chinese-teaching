Refund policy

A starter framework for refunds, cancellations and customer support.

1. Digital products

Before selling any digital product, publish a clear product-specific refund policy that explains what customers receive, when access is delivered and how to request support.

3. Consumer rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to limit consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law.

For an order or access issue, contact Mandarin Loop with your order details and a description of the problem.
